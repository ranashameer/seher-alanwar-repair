## ⚡ Architecture & Design System Highlights

### 1. UX Engineering & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
- **Fitts’s Law & Natural Thumb Zone:** Persistent mobile floating action deck (`MobileBottomDeck.tsx`) with 48px+ touch targets (45% Direct Call, 55% WhatsApp Live with 30m Arrival beacon).
- **Hick’s Law:** 3-step progressive diagnostic widget (Appliance → Symptom → Dubai Neighborhood → Instant Pre-filled WhatsApp link).
- **Jakob’s Law:** Transparent Dubai market pricing brackets with upfront inspection fee waiver policy (`AED 90` waived on repair approval).
- **Perceived Speed & CLS = 0:** Framer Motion spring micro-interactions, responsive typography, and layout shift prevention.

### 2. Design System Tokens
- **Canvas / Header:** Deep Obsidian (`#090D16`), Card Surface (`#0F172A`), Divider (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Brand Accents:** Royal Electric Blue (`#2563EB`), Tech Cyan Highlight (`#06B6D4`).
- **Conversion CTA:** High-Voltage Safety Amber/Orange (`#EA580C` & `#F97316`).
- **Social Action Color:** WhatsApp Official Emerald (`#25D366`).
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter with strict 8-point spatial rhythm.

### 3. Screen-by-Screen Features
- **Header & Navbar:** Glassmorphic navigation with active duty beacon and emergency call button.
- **Hero & Diagnostic Widget:** Split-screen value proposition with triple trust badges and instant triage generator.
- **Trust Ticker:** Dynamic 4-column metric strip (12,500+ Homes, 45 Min Arrival, 90 Days Warranty, 100% Genuine Spare Parts).
- **Brand Marquee:** Infinite scrolling compatibility marquee (Samsung, LG, Bosch, Siemens, Whirlpool, Ariston, Beko, Miele, etc.).
- **Interactive Service Catalog:** Filterable categories (Laundry, Refrigeration, Cooking, Dishwashers, AC) with detail modal.
- **Interactive Cost Estimator:** Problem severity selector with transparent labor + parts breakdown.
- **Live Dubai Coverage Area Checker:** Instant search and availability display with 30–60 min arrival estimate.
- **4-Step Customer Journey:** Transparent visual roadmap from triage to 90-day warranty certificate.
- **Verified Google Reviews:** 4.9/5 Google Rating badge and real Dubai customer testimonials.
- **SEO Schema FAQ Accordion:** Expandable answers for response times, inspection fee waiver, and warranty.
- **Contact & Location Hub:** Embedded Google Map (`25.1718887, 55.4077603`), shop hours, and direct booking inquiry form.

### 4. Local SEO & JSON-LD
Embedded Schema.org `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` structured data with exact coordinates, service area, opening hours, aggregate rating, and telephone deep links.
